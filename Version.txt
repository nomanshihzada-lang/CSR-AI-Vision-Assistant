CSR AI Vision Assistant is a powerful Android accessibility tool that leverages Google Gemini AI to help visually impaired users understand their surroundings.

How to use 'Summarize Document':
Select the 6th option ('Summarize Document'), pick an image of a complex letter, bill, or notice, and hit Process. The AI will provide a clean Executive Summary, list down crucial information, and suggest next steps.

General Tips:
- For best results, use clear images.
- Current Item mode works directly with screen reader focus.

This assistant is completely free and open for all. No data is stored or shared with any third party besides Google Gemini API.
